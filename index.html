Hello! I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics at Imperial College London, supervised by Professor Dan Crisan. My work spans deep-learning theory, high-dimensional probability and stochastic analysis.

I am interested in transformer and LLM architectures, agent systems, model evaluation and efficient inference. Recent work includes quantitative universality for deep neural networks, a decoder-only transformer built from scratch in PyTorch, and a Kaggle Silver Medal for agent-guided ONNX model optimization.

My doctoral research studies stochastic PDEs and interacting particle approximations of fluid models. Alongside the PhD, I work on applied machine learning and data science projects at Stripe Partners.
